Folio PDF fixture — page 1 of 3

This document exists so that opening a .pdf from the files column can be checked end to end: the page lane mints one file: URL, the engine's own reader draws it, and the head and the foot name the file on disk.

What to look for

  • The reader's toolbar says 1 of 3.
  • This paragraph is legible — a reader that loaded but could not reach the document draws its own "File not found" plate instead.
  • The address row shows the OS spelling of the path, not a file:/// URL.
