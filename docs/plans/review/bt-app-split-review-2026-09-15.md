## Phase 4 — the refusals, the gate-time model, and Step 0

### 4.1 What the plan refuses

**§9.2 / §8.3 — no plugin API, no ABI, no dynamic loading.** Correct, and it should stay. First, because Step 0 is first and because it is the only step in the plan that changes what ships without changing a line of source.

2. **Step 2 lands with the platform array, the 91 pins and the text relocation in one commit, inside a window where three branches in four conflict.** Findings 16 and 21 and question 9: the freeze is right, but its cost is proportional to what is inside it, and the two pieces of work that are not pure relocation can and should be finished before it opens.

3. **Step 3 is ticketed off the 66,273-line table and five of the sixteen rows turn out to be impossible mid-ticket.** Finding 15. The failure mode is not a red build — it is a ticket that quietly grows a root-item relocation and stops being a pure move, which is exactly what §9.3 forbids and what the `--list` check cannot see.
