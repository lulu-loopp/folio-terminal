**B1 remains BLOCKS: the environment exception can permanently refuse a previously correct block** (`crates/bt-detect/src/lib.rs:3211`, `:3287`; `docs/DESIGN.md:106`).
Minimal final screen: [`\begin{aligned}`, `x&=y`, `\end{aligned}`, `$$`]. Main owns the exact environment at rows 0..2; target owns nothing and reports `ClippedTail { body_start: 0, closer: 3 }`. The same happens with `a=b` appended but no closer.
There is no time/feed bound: an unchanged snapshot always gives the same answer. A producer may finish or pause forever after that `$$`; this includes a hypothetical final answer ending in an unmatched delimiter (no claim about a specific observed Claude Code run).
Appending [`a=b`, `$$`] restores both blocks, but appending [`ordinary prose here`, `$$`] does NOT restore the environment: even a printed closer is insufficient unless the forward block passes validation. The latter is a fully closed, permanent B1 regression, not just streaming latency.
This deliberate trade-off is not acceptable under the unchanged release bar. Must fix the loss of independently correct standalone environments, including stopped-output and closed-but-refused cases; require distinguishing ownership/boundary evidence or a bounded recovery policy that preserves whole-block correctness. A timer alone cannot distinguish identical rows and risks restoring the original sub-range bug. Calling this transient or documenting it does not waive the bar; the two indistinguishable readings need an explicit policy resolution.

**Floor attacks.** An environment at 0..2, `$$p=q$$` at 3, and lone `$$` at 4 leaves both complete occurrences untouched and yields no clip. Inserting `z=w` at 4 moves the lone delimiter to 5 and proves only tail 4..5; the environment still survives.
An Ineligible input/prompt `$x^2$` row is not a floor: [environment 0..2, inline-shaped row 3, `$$` 4, `a=b` 5] becomes tail 0..4, whereas CommandOutput preserves both occurrences. Correct under the occurrence/eligibility rule: there was no inline occurrence to preserve. It is not independent proof that prompt bytes belong to a display; absorbing the preceding environment is another B1 manifestation. A valid closer restores that environment; a prose-body closer does not.
Nested aligned/gathered environments are consumed whole in the clipped reading and return as one outer occurrence when the forward block closes validly. No nested sub-range was emitted in this probe.
A closed fence between two environments floors the tail after its closing marker: the first environment survives and the second is suppressed until valid forward closure. An environment inside a closed fence never renders; the tail begins below the fence. An open fence prevents clipping and rendering inside it.
With two lone `$$` rows, only the FIRST is considered for clipping. A valid intervening `a=b` makes it the ordinary opener and the second its closer, preserving the environment above. Empty or prose-only intervening content fails convergence: the first becomes the clipped closer, and the second is an unclosed opener. There is no search for a better later closer.
Phase and convergence checks are unchanged; these cases add no mismatched-raster claim. The proving/preserving wording in CHANGELOG/DESIGN is corrected; the streaming-lifetime wording still overpromises as B1 shows.

**Recorded for later — cost.** The suffix loop (`lib.rs:3253`) is one upward pass, uses a running newline-inclusive byte count and content flag, and allocates nothing; `body_line_looks_like_prose` is allocation-free and the whole-body predicate is literally `lines().any(...)`. The repeated joining cost is removed.
The helper as a whole is NOT allocation-free or simply one linear scan: it collects all logical rows, fully scans `lines[..first_dollars]` for floors (`:3199`), materializes occurrences/tables, builds a BTreeSet, and searches it for every prefix row. For P prefix rows, K hard floors, and M detected math occurrences, set work is O((P+K) log(K+1)); the added scan's table claiming loop (`:2134`) visits P rows for each of M occurrences, O(P*M), quadratic when M scales with height. Ordinary scanning and the existing forward convergence scan remain additional work.
The 8192-byte suffix limit does not bound that prefix scan or its allocations. Tall, frequently changed grids pay this extra work whenever detection is recomputed; shared `live_grid_owned_blocks` callers reuse an unchanged LiveGridAnswer (`session.rs:6516`), so it is not unconditionally paid on every unchanged feed.
