# The 0.5 design tokens and state rules

Status: **specification, revision (b), 2026-09-27.** It records the tokens and the
state rules the owner has ruled for the 0.5 interface: colour, spacing, type,
controls, the rounded-card variant, and how each class of control answers hover,
open, focus, selection and disabled. It does **not** specify any page: the where ×
what panel, the quota panel, the agent rail, the Git pages and Settings each get
their own surface spec after the owner signs that page off (§8). No product code
changes with this note.

Revision (b) brings the note through the prototype's rounds 25–29 and the owner's
rulings of 2026-09-27 (the two blues, the rounded-card edges and radii, no new grey
tier and the section labels as they are), restores the token rows that revision (a) broke, replaces family boilerplate
with verified consumers, corrects twelve contrast figures, and gives one answer per
control and state combination (§5).

**Machine-readable colour table:** `ui-05-tokens.tsv`, beside this file. One row per
**colour** token: name, light, dark, role, surface, contrast. A value is a hex, an
`rgba()` (alpha over the ground it is drawn on, §0.2), `= <token>` (an alias: the
same value as that token in that theme), or `-` (no value in that theme; §1.11 says
what that means). Non-colour tokens (spacing, sizes, radii, weights, opacities,
timings) are in §2–§4 of this note only; the TSV carries no geometry, and the
MD ↔ TSV parity promise (§1.11) covers colour tokens only.

### 0.2 Contrast, and how a figure is computed

WCAG 2.1 ratios. Floors: **4.5:1** for body text, **3:1** for a mark that carries
meaning (SC 1.4.11). Inactive controls are exempt (SC 1.4.3), and the section labels
are an owner-accepted exception (§1.2).

**Reference grounds**, always in this order:

- **light** — band/chrome `#f7f7f5` · pane and popover `#ffffff` · active rail row `#e6e6e3`;
- **dark** — band/chrome `#252525` · popover `#2a2a2a` · pane `#1b1b1b` · active rail row `#393939`.

These seven grounds are a reference matrix, not evidence for every surface. A consumer
with a floor that sits on another ground is checked on its **actual** ground in §1.10.

**Convention: two kinds of alpha.** The product has two, and each token says which it
is.

- **Token inks and plates** (`ink-*` with alpha, `row-hover`, `hairline`, `hairline-soft`, the dark `mark-gray`): composited over the ground in **8-bit sRGB** — `round(a·fg + (1 − a)·bg)` per channel, halves up. This is the product's own integer `ink_over` arithmetic that precomputes `ChromePalette` (its opaque greys are exactly these composites) and what the prototype draws. **Every contrast figure in this note uses it:** composite, then WCAG relative luminance, then the ratio rounded to two decimals. `brightness(k)` is a per-channel multiply in sRGB, rounded and clamped to 255 (what CSS does).
- **Overlays the renderer blends at draw time** (`edge-float`, `rule-menu`, the `scrim`, the picker washes, and sprite opacities such as `tool-reveal` and the .35 of a disabled mark): blended in **linear light**, because the product draws into an sRGB surface and the GPU blends there (prototype STATUS "1:1 · Palette": a 22/255 black hairline on white reads `#f5f5f5`, not `#e9e9e9`; the scrim matches the capture to ±1/255). For these the note gives the linear-light pixels. The prototype reproduces this with `color-mix(in srgb-linear, X p%, <ground>)`; a mix with `transparent` instead only scales `X`'s alpha, and the browser then composites in sRGB — so where the prototype writes the picker washes that way its pixels are not the product's (§5.6).

**Figures.** Every ratio in this note and the TSV was recomputed under that convention.
A figure marked † is one `breathing.md` also reports; where the report's figure differs
(twelve light mark ratios on `#e6e6e3`, and deep-red / deep-indigo), **this note's
recomputation is the value** and the report's is superseded (§1.5, §1.7).

### 1.3 Grey levels: how many, and why they are not collapsed

The owner asked how many greys there are. Counted as **designed levels** — not as
every pixel a translucent ink produces on every ground:

| Level | Expression | What else is the same level |
|---|---|---|
| primary | `#37352f` / white .87 | `ink-title-hover` (light identical; dark = primary on the band); `ink-vendor` (light identical; dark `#e4e4e4`, one step above primary on the band) |
| secondary | ink .65 / white .55 | `ink-title` = secondary's band composite, opaque; `ink-menu` = secondary's popover composite raised to 4.5:1 (below) |
| muted | ink .45 / white .38 | `ink-hint` and `ink-pane-title` = muted's composites on white (light `#a5a4a1`), on the popover (dark `#7b7b7b`) and on the pane (dark `#727272`) |
| disabled | `#d2d2d0` / `#525252` | exempt |

That is **three reading levels and disabled**; the section labels are the muted level.
The native palette's many fixed greys (`#716f6a`, `#74736e`, `#767570`, `#797874`,
`#9b9a96`, dark `#e1e1e1` … `#e8e8e8`, …) are **generated composites** of those three
alphas over a specific ground and state (`ChromePalette::derive`, `ink_over`); they stay
generated, are not named tiers, and are not remapped by this note.

**The safe direction (adopted): one semantic level, generated composites.** Keep one
*secondary* level and one *muted* level as the only chosen inks; every opaque value
that renders them on a particular ground (`ink-title`, `ink-menu`, `ink-hint`,
`ink-pane-title`, the dialog and files-row greys) is **generated** from the level and
its ground, not chosen. The product's menu-text correction stays in the generation:
`crates/bt-render/src/scheme.rs` raises the menu row's words to 4.5:1 with
`contrast::raise_against(ink2(menu), menu, CHROME_TEXT_MINIMUM_CONTRAST)` (user ruling
2026-08-28, `docs/DESIGN.md` §7.37), which is why light `ink-menu` is `#777672` (4.55)
and not the plain composite `#7d7c78` (4.18). This removes independently chosen greys
without pretending alpha and opaque values are interchangeable, and changes no pixel.

**Not adopted: a literal collapse of `ink-secondary`, `ink-title` and `ink-menu` into
one value.** The three are equal only on some grounds. Each composited on each named
ground:

| Theme / ground | `ink-secondary` | `ink-title` | `ink-menu` |
|---|---|---|---|
| light `#f7f7f5` | `#7a7974` · 4.07 | `#7a7974` · 4.07 | `#777672` · 4.24 |
| light `#ffffff` | `#7d7c78` · 4.18 | `#7a7974` · 4.36 | `#777672` · 4.55 |
| light `#e6e6e3` | `#74736e` · 3.80 | `#7a7974` · 3.49 | `#777672` · 3.64 |
| dark `#252525` | `#9d9d9d` · 5.65 | `#9d9d9d` · 5.65 | `#9f9f9f` · 5.79 |
| dark `#2a2a2a` | `#9f9f9f` · 5.42 | `#9d9d9d` · 5.29 | `#9f9f9f` · 5.42 |
| dark `#1b1b1b` | `#989898` · 5.97 | `#9d9d9d` · 6.35 | `#9f9f9f` · 6.51 |
| dark `#393939` | `#a6a6a6` · 4.74 | `#9d9d9d` · 4.26 | `#9f9f9f` · 4.36 |

- **All three → translucent secondary:** the band is byte-identical; light menu, combo-list, toast, field and selection-action text and the SPLIT outline lighten 6 per channel on white, **4.55 → 4.18**, under the menu floor the product enforces today. Dark menus on `#2a2a2a` are unchanged.
- **All three → `ink-menu`'s value:** band controls and tabs darken (3, 3, 2) in light and brighten 2 in dark; secondary text on white darkens 6; on the dark active row it goes `#a6a6a6` → `#9f9f9f`, **4.74 → 4.36**.
- **All three → `ink-title`'s value:** band pixels unchanged; light menu text **4.55 → 4.36**; secondary text on the dark active row **4.74 → 4.26**.

A literal collapse therefore needs an explicit changed-appearance decision from the
owner; it is listed in §7 (row 5), not taken here.

### 1.9 What may carry colour

This is the one rule every surface spec checks itself against.

1. **Neutral is the default.** Surfaces, rows, selected rows, menu rows, ordinary buttons, section labels, secondary text, and the head's three controls at rest are neutral. The badge and the quota control rest in the gear's `ink-title` (item 64); colour appears on them only for a state.
2. **The identity `accent` is for focus, objects and links** (owner, 2026-09-22; round 26): the focus ring; a link; a folder glyph and the menu's glyphs; the ✓ of a chosen row; the picker's zone; a held divider; an open tool's glyph; the primary button's fill (open, §7). Never a surface, a menu row's text, a selected tab, or a state.
3. **State is never the accent.** Done, unread, in use, the current branch, commit dots and the `main` pill are `mark-blue` (state blue), alongside the other state hues of rule 5.
4. **Meaning is never carried by colour alone.** Every coloured mark sits beside a count, a word or a shape that says the same thing: a status dot with its state word; the badge's dot with its number; the quota ring with its figure; a bar with its percentage; a chosen row with its ✓; a context ring with its percentage (0.5 note §12.3). A reader who cannot tell orange from red loses nothing.
5. **One hue, one meaning.** Orange = needs you or quota (Waiting, Limited, ≥ 80 %, used up — used up is orange and static, item 78, including the 用完 word); red = failed, nothing else; blue = done / unread / in use and the Git page's current state; gray = neutral marks; the lane hues = graph lanes only.
6. **A coloured word takes its hue's text value from §1.6** in its theme — never a hand-picked shade.
7. **Brand colours are identity, not state** (§1.8). A vendor's glyph never changes colour to show a state; the state is the dot and the word beside it.

### 1.11 Names, aliases and parity

- **Every colour token is one TSV row**, including each `text-<hue>` (= its mark in light), each `lane-N`, each `press-<hue>` and `deep-<hue>`, and the alias rows `ink-section`, `mark-gray-here`, `fill-primary`, `divider-active`, `ink-inverse`, `picker-wash-hover`, `picker-wash-chosen`, and the linear-light overlays `edge-float`, `rule-menu` (their cells give the pixel on the named ground). A surface names a token, never a raw value.
- **Alias rule.** `= <token>` in a theme's cell means that theme's value of the named token, resolved per theme (`lane-0` light = `mark-blue` light; dark has its own value). An alias is a distinct role that shares a value today; a later change to one side must decide whether the other follows.
- **`-` in a cell** means no value in that theme. For a mark or text hue it means *reserved, not drawn* in that theme (a surface that needs one comes back to the owner — §7 row 11); for a press value it means that hue has no hover in that theme; for `deep-*` it means comparison-only, light only.
- **Prototype and product names.** Prose names map to TSV names as follows: "Windows close hover" = `caption-close-hover`; the focus ring's and the moment's pane ring's colour = `accent` (geometry in §4); `--c-accent` / `--mock05-accent` = `accent`; `--mock05-err/warn/ok/pause` and `--c-status-*` = the status value of `mark-red/orange/green/yellow`; `--graph-lane-N` = `lane-N`; `--graph-lane-text-N` = `text-<lane hue>` in light, `lane-N` in dark; `--apple-press-<hue>` = `press-<hue>`; `--apple-deep-<hue>` = `deep-<hue>`; `--c-files-row-hover` = `row-hover-files`; `--c-rail-edge` = `rail-edge`; `--c-pane-head-edge` = `pane-head-edge`.
- **Parity (the acceptance criterion):** the set of colour token names in this note (§1.1–§1.8 tables, with `text-<hue>`, `lane-N` and `deep-<hue>` defined by the rules of §1.5–§1.7 and listed one per row in the TSV) equals the set of TSV names, and each pair of values is equal. Non-colour tokens are specified in §2–§4 only and are outside that criterion; a generator reads geometry from this note, and never parses a geometry string as a colour.

### 4.1 The rounded variant (圆角)

圆角 is the owner's leaning direction with 宽松 (round 27, tentative); under 紧凑
panels stay square whatever the toggle says. Its contract (`panels.css`, `tokens.css`

- **What is a card:** the files column and every pane with its head. **The sidebar is not a card** (round 25): a plain full-height column against the window's left edge, no radius, no edge, no gutter round it; only the gutter to its right separates it from the first card.
- **The ground:** cards sit on `surface-chrome` (the band's colour) with a 4-px gutter between cards and to the window's right and bottom edges.
- **The edge:** 1 px of `rail-edge` (`#e9e9e8` / `#323232`) on **all four sides of every card**, the lower two cards of a 2 × 2 grid included (round 28). - **The head separator stays** (`pane-head-edge`, 1 px under every pane head and the files head; owner ruling of round 28, which supersedes the earlier "no header separator under 宽松" proposal).
- **Rings, outlines and plates follow the card** (round 29): any ring, selection outline or hover plate that reaches a card's corner is drawn on the card's own box with the card's radius (8), never a square rectangle clipped inside it. ### 5.1 Precedence (one rule for every member)

States resolve **per property**, highest first:

**disabled > selected (chosen, active, here, pinned) > open > hover > rest**

- *Fill:* the highest active state that defines a fill wins (a selected row keeps `row-selected` under hover and while its card is open; a hovered row that is also open shows one fill, not two).
- *Ink and weight:* the highest active state that defines them wins (selected = `ink-primary` 500 wins over the hover ink).
- *Glyph colour:* open = `accent` for a control's glyph where §5.2 says so; otherwise the hover ink.
- *Keyboard focus* is not in the chain: it adds its ring on top of whatever the chain resolves, and a roving highlight (menus) is the hover state.
- *Disabled* (§5.5) removes hover, open and focus invitations; a selected disabled item keeps its selection marker.
- *A pinned card* suppresses hover only on **other agent rows** (those neither open, here nor pinned); it never suppresses hover on files rows, menus or anything else.

- **In dark the where × what rows take the menu's hover** (`#363636`), not the files column's (`#282828`): they are menu rows on the popover.

### 5.3 Focus

- **Keyboard focus** on a focusable control or row is the 2-px `accent` ring (`focus-ring`), independent of hover and selection; a field takes it inset. The ring is drawn on the host's own box, outset by its offset, with the host's corner radius plus the offset (a 6-radius control gets a 7-radius ring); inside a rounded card it follows the card (§4.1).
- **Which members take keyboard focus:** the members marked "ring" in §5.2. Menus (every menu-row member) use the roving highlight, never a ring. Readouts, caption buttons, tabs and pills draw no focus ring in the prototype or in this note; a surface spec that makes one of them keyboard-focusable gives it the ring.
- **A pane** draws no focus ring: focus is its head's mark at full strength (unfocused .5) and its title in `ink-primary` at 500 (unfocused: `ink-pane-title`, 400). A click on an agent row that sends focus to a pane rings the pane with the 2-px `accent` ring for a moment and fades it (3 × `MOTION_SLOW` 200 ms); under 圆角 that ring takes the card's radius 8 (§4.1). The pane-attention ring is that same temporary ring; there is no resting pane ring.

### 5.4 Selected

- **Row**: see §5.2. A files row and where × what's chosen folder share `row-selected-files` and the r5 rounding, **no accent bar** (item 31); a pinned place keeps its pin visible.
- **Menu row: a ✓ in the leading mark column, and the row's text in `ink-primary`; nothing else** (round 21). The ✓ is the house check (`i-check`) in the 14-wide icon column the menu's flags use, in `accent`. **No filled square, no filled pill, no radio dot anywhere** — the Git filter's All branches lost its filled accent pill for this rule. The chosen row's `ink-primary` is the product's `menu_item_text_selected`, as the combo draws it today; revision (a)'s "the row keeps the menu's ink" is withdrawn, and a menu that draws its chosen row in `ink-menu` is a prototype deviation.
- **Mac tab pill, active**: lifted on a pill ground with the lift shadow; light `#ffffff` on the `#f7f7f5` band. The dark ground is open (§7); inactive pills are flat.
- **The where × what picker's chosen side**: `accent` edge with a 22 % `accent` wash (item 21a) — a wash, never a solid block. The washes are overlays (§0.2), blended in linear light as the product blends its picker wash today (`profiles.rs PICKER_ZONE_WASH_ALPHA` .15, drawn as an alpha): on the popover, chosen light `#e5e7f7` / dark `#465486`, hover light `#eeeffa` / dark `#3e4972`. The prototype's `color-mix(in srgb-linear, accent p%, transparent)` composites in sRGB instead and shows `#d1daf6` / `#e0e6f9` (light), `#3c4259` / `#363b4a` (dark) — visibly stronger in light (§5.6).

### 5.5 Disabled

- Text `ink-disabled`; marks at .35 opacity (`UNAVAILABLE_MARK_OPACITY`), **keeping their own colour** (a brand or state mark is not greyed, only faded).
- No hover, open or focus invitation. A control that becomes disabled while its menu is open closes the menu; keyboard focus moves on.
- **Disabled + selected**: the selection marker stays (a ✓ at .35, a selected fill unchanged) so the current value stays readable; the text takes `ink-disabled`.

## 9. For the ticket that implements these tokens

What the product has today, so that ticket starts from the right place:

- **The palette lives in the renderer.** `bt-render/src/theme.rs` defines `ChromePalette`; `bt-render/src/scheme.rs` derives it (`ChromePalette::derive`) from the scheme for each theme, with separate pane, menu, dialog and chrome grounds and the menu-text contrast solver. Fixed light/dark tables are baseline and test evidence, not the runtime mechanism.
- **The persisted scheme holds twenty-one colours** (ANSI 16, background, foreground, cursor, selection, accent; `bt-persist/src/scheme.rs`), as `#rrggbb`. It cannot express alpha tokens, aliases, reserved values, the brightness rule, a card-edge key or a section-label tier. The tokens need a typed semantic layer between the scheme and `ChromePalette`, and a declared policy on which chrome roles a user scheme may change; the terminal scheme format is not the place.
- **Separate roles already exist in part:** one editable accent, four status colours, eight graph lanes, profile identity colours outside scheme control. 0.5 adds the identity / state-blue split, the Apple light mapping, the dark status/lane distinction and the neutral-mark aliases.
- **Composites survive consolidation:** keep the ground-dependent derivation and the 4.5:1 menu solver (§1.3); the section labels stay `ink-muted`, an accepted exception to the floor (§1.2).
- **Geometry, type, density, radii, focus offsets, reveal opacity and timings** are separate constants (`theme.rs`, `settings.rs`, seats, profiles, motion), not scheme colours; 圆角 needs rendering work (card clipping, the edge above contents, lower 2 × 2 edges, the ring's radius), not only a colour.
- **The TSV is an inventory, not an executable schema:** it mixes hex, `rgba()`, aliases and `-`; the implementing ticket turns it into typed tokens with availability, aliases and the actual grounds of §1.10.
- **The quota panel's account-head token is provisional.** Under 宽松 it is 13 / 500 `ink-primary` (§3); the owner dislikes the account names in full ink and the panel's look as a whole, which goes to the prototype's round 30. Do not implement that head's ink or the panel's styling from this note until round 30 settles it.
