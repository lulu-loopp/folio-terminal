# The 0.5 design tokens and state rules

Status: **specification, 2026-09-27.** It records the tokens and the state rules the
owner has ruled for the 0.5 interface: colour, spacing, type, controls, and how each
class of control answers hover, open, focus, selection and disabled. It does **not**
specify any page: the where × what panel, the quota panel, the agent rail, the Git
pages and Settings each get their own surface spec after the owner signs that page
off (§7). No product code changes with this note.

Machine-readable colour table: `ui-05-tokens.tsv`, beside this file (one row per
colour token: name, light, dark, role, surface, contrast). Its contrast column reads `L` on the three light grounds and `D` on the four dark grounds named below, in that order; `†` marks a ratio the prototype's report also measures (the two agree within 0.01).

## 0. Sources, and how to read a value

**The 0.5 design is the 0920 mock as the prototype reproduces it** (`win.html` /
`mac.html`), not the style-reference sample; the style-reference sample's spacing and
hierarchy are the reference for air and weight (owner, 2026-09-26/27). The prototype
lives outside the repository (`proto/`); every value below comes from one of:

| Short name | What it is |
|---|---|
| `tokens.css` | the prototype's only file with numbers: product constants under their own names (`--theme-*`, `--profiles-*`, …), `ChromePalette` fields (`--c-*`), the mock's own properties (`--mock05-*`), and the sections *0.5 density* and *0.5 mark colours* |
| `breathing.css`, `marks.css`, `readout.css`, `hosts.css`, `frame05.css` | the prototype's surface rules for the 0.5 frame |
| `breathing.md` | the prototype's report on the breathing pass: § 1 density, § 2 mark colours, § 3 readout hover, § 6 round 22, § 7 round 23 |
| STATUS *round N* / *item N* | the prototype's `STATUS.md`: owner review rounds (20–24 are this spec's) and numbered items |
| 0.5 note | `agent-workbench-0.5-2026-09-20.md` (this folder) — vocabulary, states, house rules |
| UI-SPEC | `docs/design/UI-SPEC.md` — the shipped look, which stays the baseline for everything this note does not change |

**Rounds that set this spec** (owner, 2026-09-26/27):

- **Round 20** — the breathing pass: a spacing scale, and 宽松 (airy) as the chrome's density.
- **Round 21** — readouts draw no plate when open either; Apple's *macOS* colour column, not iOS; the menu's selected row is a ✓, never a filled square.
- **Round 22** — light takes Apple's macOS palette for marks and coloured text; dark keeps today's colours.
- **Round 23** — coloured text in light is Apple's bright colour at every size and weight; a coloured glyph's hover/open in light is a darker value of its own hue, with no brightness filter.
- **Round 24** (2026-09-27) — round 23's 4.5:1 "deep" value read brown on hover; the hover/open tone became the hue **pressed**: 87 % of the mark's HSL lightness (`tokens.css` `--apple-press-*`, `marks.css`). *At the time of writing the prototype's code carries round 24 and its STATUS entry is still to be written; this spec follows the code.*

**Contrast.** WCAG 2.1 ratios. Floors: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for a mark that carries
meaning (SC 1.4.11). Grounds, always in this order:

- **light** — band/chrome `#f7f7f5` · pane and popover `#ffffff` · active row `#e6e6e3`;
- **dark** — band/chrome `#252525` · popover `#2a2a2a` · pane `#1b1b1b` · active row `#393939`.

A value marked † is from `breathing.md`; every other ratio was computed for this note
(the report has none for it). Values with alpha are composited over each ground first.

### 1.4 Coloured text

- **Light: a coloured word wears its mark's own Apple colour, whatever its size or weight** (round 23, ruling 1). There is no separate text value in light: `text-<hue>` = `mark-<hue>`, and the contrasts are the light column of §1.3. Every hue except indigo is under 4.5:1 on some light ground; the owner took that trade knowingly (`breathing.md` § 2, "Reading the trade").
- **Dark: a coloured word wears today's value for its hue**, the same value as the mark (under today's colours the prototype's 文字色 toggle has no effect). The five status values (red, orange, yellow, green, blue) are ≥ 4.91:1 on band, popover and pane and 3.95–4.41 on the active row; the lane values that colour ref-pill names are ≥ 4.31 on band, popover and pane (purple on the popover is the lowest) and 3.47–7.16 on the active row.

### 1.7 What may carry colour

This is the one rule every surface spec checks itself against.

1. **Neutral is the default.** Surfaces, rows, selected rows, menu rows, buttons (the primary aside, §6), section labels, secondary text, and the head's three controls at rest are neutral. The badge and the quota control rest in the gear's `ink-title` (item 64); colour appears on them only for a state.
2. **The accent is for focus, marks and links** (owner, 2026-09-22). The focus ring; the Done/unread mark; a link; the current branch's dot; the ✓ of a chosen menu row; the picker's chosen zone. Never a surface, a filled button face, a menu row's text, or a selected tab.
3. **Meaning is never carried by colour alone.** Every coloured mark sits beside a count, a word or a shape that says the same thing: a status dot with its state word; the badge's dot with its number; the quota ring with its figure; a bar with its percentage; a chosen row with its ✓; a context ring with its percentage (0.5 note §12.3). A reader who cannot tell orange from red loses nothing.
4. **One hue, one meaning.** Orange = needs you or quota (Waiting, Limited, ≥ 80 %, used up — used up is orange and static, item 78); red = failed, nothing else; blue = unread/done and the accent roles of rule 2; gray = neutral marks.
5. **A coloured word takes its hue's text value from §1.4** in its theme — never a hand-picked shade.
6. **Brand colours are identity, not state** (§1.6). A vendor's glyph never changes colour to show a state; the state is the dot and the word beside it.

### 2.2 What stays dense, and why

- **Row heights stay at the control height** — rail tabs 30, menu items 29.5, the + box 28. 32 was tried and pushed the Windows where × what panel further past a 780 window; air comes from gaps and padding instead.
- **The terminal grid** — its inset, cell metrics, font and size are measured product values (`lib.rs`), untouched.
- **The title band** — the badge, quota and gear slots, the caption cells (46 × 40), the Mac slots (36), the app title: measured values, untouched.
- **Cards mode** (`FOCUS_*`) — a measured spec with its own card rules.
- **Settings, the files column's rows, the glance and level-2 cards, the tab card** — not in the breathing pass; each keeps today's values until its own surface spec.
- **The quota panel's column widths and its 8 gap** — a wider gap eats the bar.
- **Where × what's column minimums** (250 / 200), and the line between the two columns — the one boundary that must be seen.
- **The divider-held state** — the gutter is off while a divider is held (panes inset 5 and become r8 cards, as today).

Every font size also stays as it is, except the quota panel's account head (§3).

list). An identifier is a path, a branch, a hash, a command, a model id or a time a
user may copy; everything else — titles, state words, labels, menu rows, buttons —
is the sans face.

## 5. State rules

Five classes. A surface spec names each thing it draws as one of them and inherits
these rules; it does not invent a sixth.

| Class | Members |
|---|---|
| **control** | the gear, caption buttons, the + box and its ⌄, pane-head tools, combos, buttons, fields |
| **readout** | the attention badge, the quota control (a number or a dial) |
| **row** | a rail tab row, an agent row, a files row, a where × what place row, the AGENT head |
| **pill** | the Mac tab pill, the selection pill (评论 · 翻译 · 搜索) |
| **menu row** | a row of a ⌄ menu, a context menu, a combo's list, the Git filter |

### 5.1 Hover and open

| Class | Hover | Open (its panel or menu is up) |
|---|---|---|
| control, Windows caption | `caption-plate` over the whole cell; glyph unchanged | the plate held |
| control, Mac gear | no plate; glyph `ink-title` → `ink-title-hover` (owner, 2026-09-23) | the hover ink held |
| control, pane-head tool | `tool-plate` 22 r5 | the plate held, glyph in the accent |
| control, combo | `dialog-hover` | the same, held |
| **readout** (both faces) | **no plate.** A neutral glyph goes `ink-title` → `ink-title-hover`. A coloured glyph: light `press-<hue>` (§1.5); dark the mark × 1.446. Cursor: a hand | **the hover tone held, no plate** (round 21). The popover's position says which readout owns it |
| row | `row-hover` fill, radius 6 | a row whose card is up keeps the hover fill (item 29); while a card is pinned no other row takes hover |
| pill (Mac tab) | inactive pills stay flat | — |
| menu row | `menu-row-hover`; text `ink-menu` → `ink-primary` | a row whose submenu is up: the same, held |

- **The readout rule.** The badge and the quota control are readouts, not controls: they show a value, and a plate would make them read as buttons (owner, 2026-09-26). So neither hover nor open draws a plate, on either face; the tone change is the whole response. The gear is a control and keeps its plate on Windows.
- **No brightness filter on an Apple colour** (round 23). A coloured glyph's light hover is a colour token, not a computed filter.
- **Status dots on agent rows and Git ref pills have no hover state.**

### 5.2 Focus

- **Keyboard focus** on any focusable control is the 2-px accent ring (`focus-ring`), independent of hover and selection; a field takes it inset.
- **A files row** takes a 2-px accent ring (`FILES_ROW_FOCUS_RING`).
- **A pane** draws no focus ring: focus is its head's mark at full strength (unfocused .5) and its title in `ink-primary` at 500 (unfocused: `ink-pane-title`, 400). A click on an agent row that sends focus to a pane rings the pane with the focus ring for a moment and fades it (3 × `MOTION_SLOW` 200 ms).

### 5.3 Selected

- **Row**: `row-selected` fill, `ink-primary`, weight 500. A files row and where × what's chosen folder: `row-selected-files`, the files row's own rounding, **no accent bar** (item 31); a pinned place keeps its pin visible.
- **Menu row: a ✓ in the leading mark column, nothing else** (round 21). The ✓ is the house check (`i-check`) in the 14-wide icon column the menu's flags use, in the accent; the row keeps the menu's ground and ink. **No filled square, no filled pill, no radio dot anywhere** — the Git filter's All branches lost its filled accent pill for this rule.
- **Mac tab pill, active**: lifted on a pill ground with the lift shadow; light `#ffffff` on the `#f7f7f5` band. The dark ground is open (§6); inactive pills are flat.
- **The where × what picker's chosen side**: the accent edge with a 22 % accent wash (item 21a) — a wash, never a solid block.

### 5.4 Disabled

`ink-disabled` for text; marks at .35 opacity (`UNAVAILABLE_MARK_OPACITY`); no hover,
press or focus invitation.
