# M1.5: color emoji and fallback glyph quality

## Result

The renderer now makes presentation routing explicit per terminal slot. Programmatic box/block geometry has highest priority; explicit VS16 and Unicode emoji characters outside the M1.5e bare-symbol ranges enter a mixed color-emoji route that prefers `Segoe UI Emoji` and uses the embedded `Noto Color Emoji` for missing clusters; VS15 and monochrome text symbols select `Segoe UI Symbol`; ordinary terminal text retains the Consolas/CJK chain. This avoids cosmic-text's internal name-sorted fallback order, keeps `☆` in the monochrome symbol route, and removes supported box/block characters such as `│` from font routing entirely. No glyphon, cosmic-text, swash, or other vendor source was changed.

## M1.5e visual finish

The four programmatic rounded corners now place the quarter-circle center toward the box interior and sample the opposite quadrant, so `╭╮╰╯` bulge toward their outside cell corners. Their straight arms still join the same centered horizontal and vertical lanes and reach the cell edges. Tests pin all four directions by requiring the sampled arc midpoint to be closer to the outside corner than the chord midpoint, in addition to the existing radius, continuity, and adjacency checks.

Bare font-routed media controls in `U+23EF–U+23FA` and Geometric Shapes in `U+25A0–U+25FF` use the monochrome TextSymbol route and a range-limited bidirectional fit-to-cell policy. Their raster ink is proportionally scaled up or down until one dimension fills the one-cell box, then centered by its measured ink bounds. Explicit VS16 still selects ColorEmoji, and programmatic geometry still bypasses fonts. TextSymbol characters outside those two ranges, including `☆`, retain the previous shrink-only normalization and baseline alignment; this avoids enlarging intentionally small punctuation and marks such as `·`, `•`, and `†`.

### 后续选项

将默认字体切换到 Windows 11 自带、与 Windows Terminal 同款的 Cascadia Mono，会从字体覆盖层更彻底地解决媒体控制与几何符号的格内设计问题。该选择影响默认文本观感与指标，留待用户裁决，本片不切换。

## M1.5d Windows Terminal visual alignment

The built-in colors now use Windows Terminal's **Campbell** scheme verbatim, sourced from [`microsoft/terminal`'s generated defaults](https://github.com/microsoft/terminal/blob/main/src/cascadia/TerminalSettingsModel/defaults.json): default background and explicit ANSI black are `#0C0C0C`, default foreground and ANSI white are `#CCCCCC`, and the remaining normal/bright slots use Campbell's published 16-color values. All built-in color constants live in `crates/bt-render/src/theme.rs` as the future M2 theme seam. A default-colored cell still carries `TerminalColor::Named(16/17)` and resolves through the theme foreground/background; explicit SGR 30–37/90–97 and indexed 0–15 still resolve through palette slots. Their equal Campbell values at black and white do not collapse those semantic paths.

The surface is explicitly configured to an sRGB format. Theme and palette bytes are converted once from sRGB to linear channels at the two GPU upload boundaries shared by the entire rectangle system: render-pass clear and `RectInstance` color construction. The IEC sRGB transfer function maps channel 12 to `0.003676507324...` before the surface performs its one hardware sRGB encode. Background cells, SGR 40–47 backgrounds, the cursor quad, underlines, and programmatic box/block geometry therefore share the corrected path. The block cursor uses the theme's Campbell white (`#FFFFFF`) instead of the former hard-coded slate.

Presentation intentionally departs from Unicode's default `Emoji_Presentation` behavior to match the user-selected Windows Terminal look. After the programmatic box/block bypass, an explicit selector check, and the two M1.5e bare-symbol ranges, an `Emoji=Yes` character enters ColorEmoji before Consolas coverage is considered, whether its default presentation is emoji or text. Thus naked `⚠` and `☂` are colored; bare `▶` is a fitted monochrome TextSymbol while `▶️` is color; VS15 still forces the monochrome TextSymbol route, VS16 forces color, and non-emoji `☆` is unchanged. Bare ASCII keycap components (`#`, `*`, `0`–`9`) retain the existing terminal-text exception until VS16/keycap composition expresses emoji intent.

This does not change `bt-unicode` or terminal grid accounting: naked `⚠` remains one cell. Its ColorEmoji glyph now uses a square em based on cell height, is centered over the one-cell slot, and may overhang the neighboring cell boundary like Windows Terminal. TextSymbol glyphs such as `☆` retain strict one-cell normalization. Programmatic box/block geometry continues to outrank every font route.

## Capability reconnaissance

The pinned stack is glyphon 0.12.0, cosmic-text 0.19.0, and swash 0.2.9. cosmic-text's swash raster path requests `ColorOutline(0)`, then `ColorBitmap(BestFit)`, then a normal outline. glyphon maps a returned color image to its color atlas instead of tinting it as a mask. Therefore the stack supports both the COLR/CPAL route used by Segoe UI Emoji and the bitmap-color route used by the supplied Noto asset.

`assets/fonts/NotoColorEmoji_WindowsCompatible.ttf` contains CBDT/CBLC bitmap color strikes plus GSUB. The delivered fixture sequences shape through that face as one nonzero glyph each, including the family ZWJ sequence, skin-tone sequence, and US regional-indicator pair. Raster tests observe `SwashContent::Color`; VS15 through Segoe UI Symbol observes `SwashContent::Mask`.

Windows font discovery remains bounded. The database loads the existing fixed Consolas/CJK files plus `seguiemj.ttf` and `seguisym.ttf`; it never enumerates the Fonts directory. Noto is loaded from `include_bytes!` at compile time. Embedding adds the supplied 10,682,364-byte font to the executable, but makes both `cargo test` and a standalone copied executable independent of their current working directory or a sidecar asset installation. The OFL license remains at `assets/fonts/NotoColorEmoji-LICENSE`.

## Routing, normalization, and clipping

The routing order is: supported programmatic box/block geometry first; then the last explicit VS15/VS16 selector; then bare characters in the two M1.5e fitted-symbol ranges; then Unicode `Emoji=Yes` (apart from the bare ASCII keycap-component exception); then primary-font coverage; then other non-emoji characters in the terminal symbol block; finally the terminal monospace family. Thus `☂` and `☂️` are color, `☂︎`, `☆`, `⏵`, and bare `▶` are monochrome symbols, while `│` bypasses shaping and the glyph atlas. The M1.5e ranges alone use bidirectional cell fitting; `☆` keeps its shrink-only behavior from `381e069`.

## 程序化盒线

盒线和块元素直接复用 renderer 的 instanced-quad 矩形管线，不生成程序化位图，也不进入 cosmic-text shaping 或 glyphon atlas。选择独立 quad 的原因是这些图形本来就是格几何：端点可直接使用 `cell_bounds_px` 的物理像素边界，颜色继续取每格解析后的前景色，背景、光标、下划线和既有 per-cell damage/行快照语义均不需要另建缓存或失效协议。每帧本来就会重建背景/光标矩形实例，程序化图形随同一批实例提交。

| 范围 | 程序化覆盖 |
|---|---|
| `U+2500–U+254F` | 轻线、粗线、轻/粗混合连接、二/三/四段虚线全套 |
| `U+2550–U+256C` | 双线及单/双混合角、T 形和交叉全套 |
| `U+256D–U+2570` | 四枚圆角，沿四分之一圆细分采样并以直臂连接格边 |
| `U+2574–U+257F` | 半臂及轻/粗混合半臂全套 |
| `U+2580–U+259F` | 全部八分块、半块、象限块、整块与三档阴影；阴影采用稳定的 4 像素周期点阵 |

轻线宽为 `max(1, round(font_size_px / 16))` 个物理像素，粗线为轻线的 2 倍，双线为“轻线 + 轻线间隙 + 轻线”。所有直线跨度先量化为整数物理像素，再围绕格中心选择整数边界；因此奇偶格宽/线宽组合不会落在半像素上。圆角半径取横纵可用半径的较小值，弧长每物理像素至少两个采样段，因此 DPI 增大时细分密度同步增加；弧线两端由中心线宽一致的直臂延伸到格边。直线的水平臂覆盖完整格宽，竖直臂覆盖完整格高；相邻格共享同一个边界坐标，行高包含的额外行距也被竖线墨迹覆盖，不再受字体设计高度限制。分数块按实际格宽/格高的 1/8 边界四舍五入，`█` 严格等于整格。

回落清单只有 `U+2571`（右上至左下对角线）、`U+2572`（左上至右下对角线）和 `U+2573`（对角交叉）；这三枚继续走字体路径。覆盖测试逐码点断言该清单，避免范围静默扩大或缩小。

## 混合路由

进入 ColorEmoji 分支后，渲染器先用 `Segoe UI Emoji` 对整簇做一次候选 shape。单 glyph 候选继续要求 glyph 非零、来自 Segoe、光栅内容为 `SwashContent::Color` 且墨迹宽高可归一进目标槽。多 glyph 候选还要求每个 glyph 都满足这些字体/颜色条件，并且按 layout 定位后的联合墨迹实际落在双格槽内；任一条件不满足便用内嵌的 `Noto Color Emoji` 重新 shape。

家庭序列 `👨‍👩‍👧‍👦` 最终采用 **Segoe 组合**。150% 侦察得到四个彩色 glyph：两个有 advance 的主体和两个 `w=0` 的覆盖层；覆盖层分别与主体共享定位，而不是四个人物等宽横排。联合墨迹 x 范围为 3–26px，完整落在 28px 双格槽内，自动测试也钉住四 glyph、全部彩色、同一 Segoe family 和共同入槽。`👍🏽`、`☂️` 仍使用 Segoe；本机 Segoe 国旗候选不满足门槛，`🇺🇸` 仍使用 Noto 的单个彩色 glyph。没有 Segoe 时家庭序列也稳定回退到 Noto 单 glyph。

`seguiemj.ttf` 仍以可选文件方式加载。若系统没有 Segoe UI Emoji，字体数据库探测失败后会直接让所有 ColorEmoji 簇走 Noto，不做候选 shape、不 panic，整体行为退化为 `381e069`。这是有意的可移植性边界：Noto 仍是随程序内嵌且始终可用的正确性后备。

候选决定和最终 Buffer 一起放入按 `(text, bold, italic)` 建键的整形缓存。命中只复用缓存 Buffer，既不重复字体可用性查询，也不重复候选 shape 或光栅探测；DPI/字号变化会清空缓存。miss 的额外代价仅限 ColorEmoji：Segoe 可用时至多多一次 Segoe 整形和一次 glyph 光栅检查；候选通过时该 Buffer 直接成为最终结果，没有第二次 Segoe 整形；候选失败时再做原有的 Noto 整形。系统没有 Segoe 时只做轻量的字体库可用性检查，随后成本与全 Noto 基线相同。窄/宽缓存分别有 1024/256 个 LRU 条目，颜色和网格位置不进入键。

已知观感是同一行可能出现风格混搭：Fluent 的人物、家庭或物件会邻着 Google/Noto 风格的旗帜。这是“原生 Segoe 优先、覆盖缺口由 Noto 补齐”的预期取舍，不代表颜色或占格退化。

Wide glyphs retain M1's two-cell slot: cosmic-text receives a two-cell monospace em target and glyphon clips the `TextArea` to exactly those two cells. In mode 2027 the four acceptance emoji sequences each occupy one two-cell lead/spacer pair. Legacy continues to obey its scalar grid accounting.

For a narrow slot, sizing now follows the explicit presentation route. ColorEmoji uses `cell_height_px` as its font size, centers raster ink horizontally and vertically over the owning cell, and remains row-clipped so its square ink may cross vertical cell boundaries. TextSymbol glyphs in `U+23EF–U+23FA` and `U+25A0–U+25FF` proportionally fit their measured raster ink to one cell in either direction and center it; other TextSymbol and non-primary fallback routes retain the prior shrink-only em normalization with a 5% (minimum one physical pixel) side bearing and baseline alignment. Any primary Consolas glyph stays at its natural em, preserving italic and accent bearings.

The fallback scale is not a separate cache-key input. For a live renderer, `(text, bold, italic)`, the fixed database, and current metrics determine both font IDs and scale; DPI/font-size changes already clear the cache. Color and grid column still do not affect shaping.

## Automated and visual acceptance

Renderer tests pin the exact Campbell defaults and all 16 ANSI values, separate default-color and explicit-palette resolution paths, channel 12's linear value at both clear and rect upload boundaries, and the shared Campbell-white cursor color. Glyph tests pin programmatic-before-font routing, explicit `#`/`*`/`1` TerminalText routing, naked one-cell `⚠` as a cell-height-sized centered color glyph, `⚠︎`/`☂︎` as monochrome VS15 glyphs, fitted and centered `⏵`/`▶` ink at 100%–200% DPI, and unchanged shrink-only `☆`. Geometry tests pin the exact shapes of `─`, `│`, `█`, `▀`, `▄`, `▌`, `▐`, DPI-scaled quarter-circle samples, all four rounded-corner directions, rounded-corner arm continuity, and zero-gap horizontal/vertical adjacency. They also retain embedded Noto presence, optional Segoe degradation, the four-glyph Segoe family composition and Noto flag fallback, cache-hit probe suppression, the two-cell em, narrow TextSymbol containment, ambiguous width 1, and the unchanged primary italic em. The app test parses `glyph-probe-input.vt` and still pins family/skin/flag/VS16 at two cells and VS15/`☆`/`│` at one cell each. Existing M1 width tests and their fourteen-row direct-grid baseline remain unchanged.

For coordinator pixel review, follow `scripts/dev/glyph-probe-input.md`, then run Claude Code in `bt-app` at 150% scaling. The visual verdict remains: color is visible, bars align with rulers, the flag is the US flag, and `☆` ink does not cross its one-cell markers; additionally, stacked `│` cells and the Claude Code welcome frame must have no row-boundary gaps.
